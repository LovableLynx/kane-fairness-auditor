          How to read this, honestly
          
    
            
  • One CV is a spot check, not an audit. The full audits test 10 candidates × 8 names on each tool, and Kane CLI checked all 240 readings in a real browser.
    • 
            
  • Only the name, the email address and he/she pronouns change between versions. Everything else is your CV, word for word. Click any name to see its version.
    • 
            
  • The score itself means little. The demo tools' models were trained on FairCVdb, a research dataset, so they reward odd things (Spanish, reference letters). The question is only whether the score moves when the name does.
    • 
            
  • The names come from the Bertrand & Mullainathan (2004) correspondence study. "Changed its mind" means the shortlist decision differs between versions; "score moved" means scores differ by more than 0.05, the audit's threshold.
    • 
            
  • This check runs in Python on our server, through each tool's own form. Kane runs use our prepared test CVs only.
