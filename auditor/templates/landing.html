“Nothing on the CV was different. Only the name.”

AI now reads CVs before people do. The tools that screen real applicants don't come with a warning label, and the people they reject never find out why.

So we asked one question: could anyone catch a biased hiring AI from the outside, with no access to its code? We built TalentScreen with a bias hidden inside, then tried.

The method

How we test it

The way a recruiter would, through the website.

  1. 01

    One CV, eight names

    Men and women from two name groups. Only the name, email and he/she change.

  2. 02

    A placebo

    A second name from the same group. If that moves the result too, the tool reacts to any name.

  3. 03

    Kane CLI applies

    Kane CLI from TestMu AI opens the real site, submits every CV and reads back each score.

  4. 04

    A verdict

    PASS, BIASED or NAME-UNSTABLE, with evidence. A failing tool is blocked from release.

Then an AI fixed it.
